# A Path of Our Own

A game made for Hack Club's Horizons Polaris hackathon!

THEME: "Every traveler leaves a trail across the heavens"

(I was really sleep deprived so this theme won't be very narratively well)
In this game, you play as a group of hikers and adventurers who want to reach the summit of a small peak.
The forest leading to it is uncharted and very long, and you do not have enough resources
to go straight through. You play as individual travelers who embark on 2-week expeditions into the 
forest to find a safe and fast path through it. While the dense fog and difficulty of seeing through 
the forest makes it hard to see paths from a distance, you can place flags that can be 
seen through the fog to indicate to the next traveler where to go and where to 
avoid. Your goal is to eventually trial-and-error your way through the forest.

I interpreted this theme as about the ability of peoples' experiences and their paths creating trails that 
we can walk through (their experience guiding us through challenging tasks). My initial plan was to have 
an actual text box to type notes into, but that's very tedious and breaks up the flow of the game. I'm still 
quite upset about how repetitive the core gameplay is, but I was inspired by the slow, steady feeling of progress 
from dying repeatedly and reinforcing decisions in Celeste. Hopefully this game can do the same. 

## Instructions
WASD/Arrow keys to move
SPACEBAR to place a flag
E to rotate through flag colors
Q to gather wild supplies
